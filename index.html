Technology Journey

I am Luis Eduardo Brito, a software engineer and founder based in Sao Paulo. My work sits at the intersection of financial infrastructure, cloud systems, and network security.

How I Got Here

I started as a freelance web developer, then moved through internships, consulting, and product companies. Over time I shifted from writing individual features to designing platforms that other teams could build on.

That path includes open banking, payment products, and cloud-native services. The through line is the same: take a hard operations problem and turn it into software that people can trust.

What I Care About in Software

  • Reliable systems that stay available under real traffic
  • Clear APIs that other teams can adopt without guesswork
  • Security as a default, not a later patch
  • Small, well-led teams that ship and learn quickly

Current Work in Financial Software

I am now focused on how payment and banking platforms stay reliable as they grow. That includes clear APIs, cloud operations, and the habits needed to keep those systems safe for the teams that depend on them.

  1. Open banking and payment products that other companies can adopt
  2. Cloud infrastructure that stays available under real traffic
  3. Security reviews built into everyday engineering work

What I Want to Keep Learning

The next stretch of my work is less about collecting titles and more about depth. I want to keep building production systems that stay reliable, private, and understandable to the people who run them.
